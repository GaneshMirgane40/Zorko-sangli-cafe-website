## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-octane, tech-forward, and appetizing visual identity tailored for modern vegetarian smart cafés. The personality balances youthful vitality, lightning-fast casual dining efficiency, and trust in purity. 

Drawing from **High-Contrast Bold** aesthetics infused with tactile warmth:
- **Tone:** Electric, welcoming, hygienic, and unapologetically flavorful.
- **Audience:** Gen-Z students, fast-moving professionals, and families seeking delicious, 100% vegetarian culinary indulgence with contactless smart ordering.
- **Visual Tenets:** Generous saturated accents against rich, deep charcoal surfaces or warm off-white canvas spaces, punchy micro-interactions, explicit dietary signaling, and unmistakable visual hierarchy built for quick scanning under outdoor sunlight or ambient café lighting.

## Colors

The palette revolves around appetizing warmth and clarity:
- **Primary (`#F15A24`):** Saturated culinary orange driving immediate primary action buttons, key callouts, and hot promotional banners.
- **Secondary (`#FDB813`):** Radiant golden yellow used for value badges, rating indicators, offers, and high-energy accents.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`):** Pure plant-based green reserved exclusively for the 100% vegetarian trust marker, freshness tags, and checkout success feedback.
- **Neutral (`#1A1A1A`):** Deep charcoal acting as high-contrast body text, structural dark cards, and contrasting bottom bars. Never rely on dead `#000000` for text.
- **Extended Accents:** `smart-cyan` (`#04ADED`) serves as an auxiliary cue for smart-tech capabilities (QR ordering, Wi-Fi, loyalty status), while `surface-warm-cream` (`#FFFBF5`) softens elevated culinary backdrops.

## Typography

Typography establishes an energetic cadence between geometric modernity and immediate legibility:
- **Headlines & Prices (Sora):** The rounded geometric skeleton of Sora injects technological precision and youthful bounce. Numeric prices rendered in Sora Display/Title bold weight provide immediate recognition in the local currency (`₹`).
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Friendly, warm, and hyper-legible at small screen sizes. Used for menu descriptions, allergen notes, smart status badges, and action labels.
- **Currency & Formatting:** Always position `₹` flush to the numerical figure without full spaces (e.g., `₹149`).

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid grid is optimized for high-speed touch interactions and quick category jumps:
- **Grid Structure:**
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout, 16px margins, 16px gutters. Single column for item details, 2-column grid for standard menu item listings.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, 24px margins, 20px gutters. Optimized for table-side digital kiosks and iPad registers.
  - **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout bounded by a max-width container of `1280px` with 40px outer canvas margins.
- **Touch Targets:** Minimum interactive touch target is 48px × 48px to prevent mis-clicks during hurried mobile orders.
- **Horizontal Scrolling:** Menu category pills and fast-order suggestions must use edge-to-edge overflow carousel ribbons with snapping on handheld devices.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages ambient colored lighting and tonal surface contrast over sterile grey dropshadows:
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat base surface using clean `#FFFBF5` or `#FFFFFF`.
- **Level 1 (Menu Cards & Chips):** Layered via a 1px border `rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.06)` paired with a soft warm ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(241, 90, 36, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Active States, Floating Action Baskets):** Elevated floating layers featuring `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(241, 90, 36, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Customizer Drawers):** High-impact sheet projection: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.22)`.
- **Atmospheric Highlights:** Hot hero promotional modules feature an energetic internal radial glow (`radial-gradient(circle at top right, rgba(253, 184, 19, 0.25), transparent 70%)`).

## Shapes

The geometric form language communicates friendliness, accessibility, and playful gastronomy:
- **Default Elements (`0.5rem / 8px`):** Checkboxes, form fields, and secondary metadata containers.
- **Cards & Plates (`1rem to 1.5rem / 16px to 24px`):** Food item cards, meal deal combos, and bottom sheets adopt friendly, ultra-smooth `rounded-2xl` corners.
- **Pills (`9999px`):** Category filters, vegetarian status capsules, CTA stepper buttons, and floating cart bars are full-pill silhouettes to maximize tactile tap affordance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Order CTA):** Full pill shape, solid `#F15A24` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `label-lg` styling. Subtle press downscale animation (`scale: 0.97`). Hover shifts to `#E64A19`.
- **Secondary (Customise / View):** Warm off-white or transparent fill with a crisp 1.5px border in `#F15A24`, text in `#F15A24`.
- **Add Stepper (+ / -):** High-contrast interactive pill containing minus, numeric count, and plus icons. Resting background `#FFFBF5`, border 1px solid `#F15A24`.

### Menu Cards
- Built using `rounded-2xl` (Level 1 elevation), white canvas background, and vertical layout on mobile or 2-column horizontal split.
- **Top Zone:** Food imagery with an inset absolute top-left vegetarian mark, top-right spicy/bestseller badge.
- **Body:** Item title in `headline-sm`, short ingredient preview in `body-sm` (`#666666`).
- **Footer:** Prominent price in `title-price` (`₹149`), paired with an immediate "ADD +" pill button.

### Vegetarian Indicator (Brand Seal)
- Square border of 16px × 16px with rounded corners (3px radius) in `#15803D` (1.5px border).
- Centered solid green dot (`#15803D`) with an 8px diameter.
- Accompanying text badge: Pill tag featuring light emerald background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`), green text (`#15803D`), displaying "100% VEG".

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Horizontal scrollable row.
- **Unselected:** Transparent background, 1px border `#E5E7EB`, text `#1A1A1A`.
- **Active:** Solid `#1A1A1A` or `#F15A24` fill, text `#FFFFFF`, accompanied by an active dot or micro-icon.

### Input Fields & Steppers
- Height 48px, rounded corner `0.75rem` (12px), background `#F7F8FA`, border 1px solid `rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.12)`.
- Focus state triggers a 1.5px perimeter ring in `#F15A24` and shifts background to pure `#FFFFFF`.

### Smart Café Specialized Components
- **Floating Cart Banner:** Sticky bottom bar, pill-shaped, deep charcoal `#1A1A1A` body with contrasting gold `#FDB813` count pill and orange `#F15A24` checkout action button.
- **Customization Bottom Sheet:** Smooth `rounded-t-3xl` modal displaying crust, topping, and spice-level toggles with real-time dynamic total updating.
